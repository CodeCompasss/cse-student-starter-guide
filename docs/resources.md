# 📚 Resources for CSE Students (Expanded Edition)

A curated, high-quality list of books, courses, websites, tools, and GitHub repositories.

---

## 📚 Books (Keep these as your core)

### Programming & CS Fundamentals

* *Introduction to Algorithms* – Cormen (CLRS)
* *Clean Code* – Robert C. Martin
* *The Pragmatic Programmer* – Hunt & Thomas
* *Computer Systems: A Programmer’s Perspective* – Bryant & O’Hallaron

### Data Structures & Algorithms

* *Algorithm Design Manual* – Steven Skiena
* *Data Structures and Algorithms in Java* – Goodrich
* *Grokking Algorithms* – Aditya Bhargava (beginner friendly)

### Operating Systems & Networking

* *Operating System Concepts* – Silberschatz
* *Modern Operating Systems* – Tanenbaum
* *Computer Networking: A Top-Down Approach* – Kurose & Ross
