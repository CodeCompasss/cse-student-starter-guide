# ⏳ Study Abroad Timeline (Fall Intake)
### "When to Start?"

Most students miss the deadline because they don't know the process takes **1 Year**.
If you want to join in **August 2026 (Fall)**, you must start in **August 2025**.

---

## 📅 The 1-Year Roadmap

### August - September (Preparation)
*   **GRE/GMAT:** Study and take the exam. If score is low, retake immediately.
*   **Shortlisting:** Pick 8-10 universities (3 Ambitious, 3 Moderate, 3 Safe).

### October - November (Documentation)
*   **TOEFL/IELTS:** Take the English test (it's easy, just get it done).
*   **SOP (Statement of Purpose):** Write your story. Draft 1 -> Draft 10.
*   **LOR (Letters of Rec):** Annoy your professors to upload the letters.

### December - January (Applications)
*   **Deadlines:** Most US Top 50 colleges have deadlines in Dec/Jan.
*   **Submit:** Pay the application fees ($50-$100 per college).

### March - April (Results)
*   You will start getting "Accept" or "Reject" emails.
*   **Acceptance:** Choose ONE college and pay the deposit.

### May - July (Visa & Logistics)
*   **I-20 Form:** The college sends you this proof of admission.
*   **Visa Interview (F1):** The scariest part. Prove you have money and intent to study.
*   **Flight Tickets:** Book early to save money.

---

## 💰 The "Hidden" Costs of Applying
*   **Exams (GRE+TOEFL):** ₹25,000
*   **Application Fees (8 colleges):** ₹60,000
*   **Score Reporting:** ₹10,000
*   **Counseling (Optional):** ₹20,000+
*   **Total:** Expect to spend **~₹1 Lakh** just on the application process.
