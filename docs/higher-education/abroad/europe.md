# 🇪🇺 Masters in Europe (Germany, UK, etc.)
### "Quality Life & Low Fees"

If the US sounds too expensive or risky, Europe is the alternative.

---

## 🇩🇪 Germany: The Free Education Haven
*   **Tuition:** **ZERO** (at most public universities). You only pay living expenses.
*   **Language:** Courses are in English, but you **MUST learn German** (A2/B1 level) to integrate socially and get local jobs.
*   **APS Certificate:** A new mandatory document for Indian students to verify grades.

## 🇬🇧 United Kingdom (UK)
*   **Duration:** 1 Year Masters (saves time).
*   **Cost:** High (similar to US).
*   **Visa:** 2 Year PSW (Post Study Work) visa allows you to look for jobs after graduating.

## 🇳🇱 Netherlands / 🇸🇪 Sweden
*   **Tech Hubs:** Amsterdam and Stockholm are massive tech hubs (Booking.com, Spotify).
*   **Culture:** High English proficiency. Very expensive living costs.
