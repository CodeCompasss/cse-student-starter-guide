# 💰 Scholarships for Study Abroad
### "Don't pay full price."

Studying abroad is expensive, but you don't always have to pay for it.

---

## 🌍 The "Free Ride" Scholarships
These are fully funded (Tuition + Flight + Stay). Extremely competitive.

### 1. Erasmus Mundus (Europe)
*   **What:** You study in 2-3 different European countries over 2 years.
*   **Benefits:** 100% Free + €1000 monthly stipend.
*   **Requirement:** High CGPA + Work Experience helps.

### 2. DAAD (Germany)
*   **What:** The German government pays you to study there.
*   **Focus:** Strong preference for students with 2 years of work experience.

### 3. Fulbright-Nehru (USA)
*   **What:** For Master's in US.
*   **Catch:** You MUST return to India after graduating. (Not good if you want to settle in US).

---

## 🇮🇳 Indian Trusts (Loan Scholarships)
They give you an interest-free loan (you pay back only the principal amount).

### 4. JN Tata Endowment
*   **Amount:** ₹1 Lakh to ₹10 Lakh.
*   **Process:** Online Application -> Test -> Interview.

### 5. KC Mahindra
*   **Amount:** Up to ₹10 Lakh (Interest-free loan).

---

## 🏫 University Specific (The Most Common)
Most universities give "entrance scholarships" automatically based on your profile.
*   **Merit Scholarship:** $2,000 - $10,000 discount on tuition.
*   **TA/RA (Teaching/Research Assistant):** You work for a professor ~20 hours/week.
    *   **Benefit:** Often waives your ENTIRE tuition fee + gives a monthly salary.
    *   **How:** Email professors *before* you join.

---

## 💡 Pro Tip
**Don't pay for scholarship searches.** Use free portals like `WeMakeScholars` or the University's own financial aid page.
