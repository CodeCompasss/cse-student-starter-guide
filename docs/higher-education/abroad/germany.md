# 🇩🇪 Masters in Germany
### "High Tech, Zero Tuition"

Germany is an engineering paradise. But it is rigorous and bureaucratic.

---

## 🏫 Types of Universities
1.  **TU (Technical Universities):** *TU Munich, RWTH Aachen, TU Berlin.*
    *   **Focus:** Theory, Research. Hard to pass exams. Prestigious.
2.  **FH (Fachhochschule / Applied Sciences):**
    *   **Focus:** Practical, internship-based. Good for finding jobs directly.

---

## 📝 The Application Process (Complex)
1.  **APS Certificate:** You MUST submit your B.Tech docs to the German Embassy (APS India) for verification. This takes 1-2 months. Do this first.
2.  **Uni-Assist:** Most colleges accept applications via the `uni-assist` portal.
3.  **VPD:** Some colleges need a Preliminary Review Document (VPD).

---

## 🗣️ The Language Barrier (The Truth)
*   **"Can I study in English?"** Yes. Many masters are 100% English.
*   **"Can I get a job with English only?"** Maybe in Berlin startups. But for Bosch, Siemens, BMW? **NO.**
*   **Rule:** Start learning German in India. Reach **B1 Level** before you fly.

---

## 💰 Expenses (Blocked Account)
*   **Tuition:** €0 (mostly).
*   **Blocked Account (Sperrkonto):** You must deposit ~€11,208 (changes yearly) into a German bank to prove you can survive for 1 year. You get this back monthly (~€934/month) for rent/food.

---

## 🏭 Part-Time Work (Hiwi / Werkstudent)
*   **Werkstudent:** You work at a tech company for 20 hours/week.
*   **Pay:** €15 - €20/hour.
*   **Benefit:** This experience counts heavily for full-time jobs.
