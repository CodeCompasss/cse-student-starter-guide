# 📝 Entrance Exams: The Complete Guide
### "GRE, TOEFL, IELTS, Duolingo - decoded."

Do not waste money writing the wrong exam. Read this first.

---

## 🗣️ English Proficiency (Mandatory for Everyone)
You must prove you can speak English. You have 3 options.

### 1. TOEFL (Test of English as a Foreign Language)
*   **Accepted By:** 100% of US Universities. Preferred in USA.
*   **Format:** Reading, Listening, Speaking, Writing. (Academic focus).
*   **Score Target:** 100+/120.
*   **Cost:** ~$190.

### 2. IELTS (International English Language Testing System)
*   **Accepted By:** UK, Canada, Australia, Europe (Preferred). Most US colleges accept it too now.
*   **Format:** Reviewers talk to you face-to-face (Speaking section).
*   **Score Target:** 7.5+ Band (No section less than 6.5).
*   **Cost:** ~₹17,000.

### 3. Duolingo English Test (DET)
*   **The Cheat Code:** It is cheaper ($60), shorter (1 hour), and you can take it from home.
*   **Acceptance:** Many US universities (ASU, NEU) accept it now. **Check the university website first.**

### ❓ "Is IELTS Mandatory?"
*   **For USA:** No. TOEFL is better.
*   **For UK/Canada:** **YES.** It is strictly required for the Visa process (SDS Category in Canada).
*   **Waiver:** Some US universities waive the English test if you prove your B.Tech was in English. (Rare, don't count on it).

---

## 🧠 GRE (Graduate Record Examination)
It tests your Math (Quant) and English (Verbal) aptitude.
*   **Is it Waived?** After COVID, many top US universities made GRE "Optional".
    *   *Strategic Advice:* If you have a low GPA (< 8.0), **Take the GRE** and score 320+. It balances your profile.
    *   If you have 9.0+ GPA, check if the uni is "GRE Waived". You might save $220.
*   **Target Score:**
    *   **Quant:** 165+/170 (Crucial for CS).
    *   **Verbal:** 150+/170 (Just pass).
    *   **AWA:** 3.5+/6.0.

## 🇩🇪 GATE (For Germany/Singapore)
*   **NUS/NTU (Singapore):** They accept GATE scores for M.Tech!
*   **TU Munich (Germany):** Some courses ask for GATE scores as proof of technical skill.
