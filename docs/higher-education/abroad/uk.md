# 🇬🇧 Masters in UK (United Kingdom)
### "Fast Degree, High Cost, Good Visa"

The UK offers the fastest route to a Master's degree (1 Year), but the economy is currently fluctuating.

---

## ⏱️ The 1-Year Advantage
*   **USA/Canada:** Masters takes 2 years.
*   **UK:** Masters takes **1 Year (12 months)**.
*   **Benefit:** You save 1 year of living expenses and start earning salary sooner.

---

## 🎫 The Graduate Route Visa (PSW)
*   After graduating, you get a **2-Year Post Study Work (PSW) Visa**.
*   You can work *any* job during this time.
*   **Challenge:** Getting a "Skilled Worker Visa" Sponsorship after 2 years is hard because minimum salary thresholds have increased (as of 2024).

---

## 🏫 Russell Group Universities (The "Ivy League" of UK)
Focus on these for best brand value:
1.  **Oxbridge:** Oxford, Cambridge (Elite).
2.  **Imperial College London:** World class for CS.
3.  **UCL (University College London):** AI powerhouse.
4.  **University of Edinburgh:** Strong Informatics/AI.
5.  **Manchester / Bristol / Warwick.**

---

## 💰 Cost Breakdown
*   **Tuition:** £25,000 - £35,000 (₹25L - ₹37L).
*   **London Living:** Extremely expensive (£1500/month rent).
*   **Non-London:** Much cheaper (£800/month).

---

## ⚠️ The "September vs January" Intake
*   **September:** The main intake. All top colleges open.
*   **January:** Secondary intake. Fewer options. **Avoid if possible** as it aligns poorly with graduate job schemes.
