# 🦅 Masters in USA (MS CS)
### "The Silicon Valley Dream"

The US is still the headquarters of Tech. If you want to work at the absolute cutting edge (and earn in Dollars), this is the way.

---

## 🇺🇸 Types of Degrees
1.  **MS CS (Thesis):** Research-focused. Good if you want a PhD later.
2.  **Professional MS (MCS):** Job-focused. No thesis, just coursework. Good for getting a job quickly.

---

## 💰 The Cost Reality
*   **Tuition:** $40,000 - $80,000 (2 Years).
*   **Living:** $1500/month.
*   **Total Loan:** ₹50 Lakh - ₹1 Crore.
*   **ROI:** Most graduates earn $100k+ (₹80 Lakh) per year. You can pay off the loan in 2-3 years.

---

## 🗝️ Key Requirements
1.  **GRE:** Score 320+ (Quant 165+ is critical for CS).
2.  **TOEFL / IELTS:** English proficiency.
3.  **SOP (Statement of Purpose):** Your story. Why US? Why this college?
4.  **LORs (Letters of Recommendation):** 3 letters from professors/managers.

---

## 🚧 The H1B Visa Hurdle
*   **OPT (Optional Practical Training):** After graduating, you get **3 Years** to work in the US on your student visa (STEM OPT).
*   **H1B Lottery:** After 3 years, you need an H1B visa. It is a lottery system (luck-based).
*   **Green Card:** As an Indian, the wait time is 100+ years. Most people work on H1B forever or move back.

> **Bottom Line:** Go for the money and exposure, not for citizenship.
