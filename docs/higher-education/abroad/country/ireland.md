# 🇮🇪 Masters in Ireland
### "The Silicon Valley of Europe"

Why is Ireland special? Because **Google, Meta, Apple, Stripe, and Microsoft** have their European Headquarters in Dublin (for tax reasons). This means JOBS.

---

## 🍀 The Critical Skills Visa
*   **Stay Back:** 2 Years post-study visa.
*   **Critical Skills List:** "ICT Professionals" (Developers) are on the critical list. This makes getting a work permit very easy compared to UK/Europe.

---

## 🏫 Top Colleges
1.  **Trinity College Dublin (TCD):** The most prestigious (Hogwarts vibes).
2.  **UCD (University College Dublin):** Very modern, business-focused.
3.  **DCU (Dublin City Uni):** Good for industry connections.

---

## 💰 Cost vs ROI
*   **Tuition:** €15,000 - €25,000 (Cheaper than UK/US).
*   **Duration:** 1 Year.
*   **Jobs:** High demand for Software Engineers, Data Scientists, and Cloud Engineers.

---

## ⚠️ The Housing Crisis
*   **Dublin is full.** Finding an apartment is the hardest part of studying in Ireland.
*   Reliable accommodation is expensive (€1000+ for a room). Start looking months in advance.

---

## 🇪🇺 EU Access
*   Even though Ireland is in the EU, it is **not** in Schengen. You cannot travel visa-free to Paris/Germany directly without a separate visa (usually). *Check current rules.*
*   But it is the only English-speaking country left in the EU (post-Brexit).
