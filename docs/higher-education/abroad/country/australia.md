# 🇦🇺 Masters in Australia
### "High Wages, laid back life"

Australia is known for high quality of life and extremely high minimum wages. It is great for techies who want a work-life balance.

---

## 🦘 The PR System (Points Based)
Australia wants skilled migrants.
*   **Age:** Under 30 gets max points.
*   **English:** 8.0+ in IELTS/PTE gets max points.
*   **Education:** Masters in STEM/IT gives you points.
*   **Regional Area:** Studying in Perth/Adelaide (instead of Sydney/Melbourne) gives extra points for PR.

---

## 🏫 Group of Eight (Go8)
These are the top universities (Research focused):
1.  **University of Melbourne**
2.  **ANU (Australian National University)**
3.  **UNSW (Sydney):** Very strong Engineering/CS.
4.  **University of Sydney**

---

## 💰 Cost & Work
*   **Tuition:** AUD 40,000 - 50,000 per year. (Expensive).
*   **Part-Time Work:** You can earn AUD 24/hour (minimum wage). Students save a LOT of money just by working part-time.

---

## ⚠️ Recent Changes (2024 Migration Strategy)
*   **Visa Rejections:** Australia has tightened student visas for "high risk" countries. Ensure your financial docs are genuine.
*   **Post-Study Work:** 2 years (usually). Extended depending on the degree/region.

---

## 🌞 The Climate Factor
*   Unlike Canada/Europe, the weather is warmer (similar to India in many parts).
*   If you hate the cold, choose Australia.
