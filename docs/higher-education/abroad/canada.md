# 🇨🇦 Masters in Canada
### "The Pathway to Permanent Residency (PR)"

Canada is the #1 choice for students who value **immigration** over prestige. It is easier to settle there than in the US, but the job market is smaller.

---

## 🍁 The PR Advantage
*   **PGWP (Post-Graduation Work Permit):** Study for 2 years, get a 3-year open work permit.
*   **PR Points:** You get extra points for a Canadian degree. Most techies get PR within 2-3 years of working.

---

## 🏫 Types of Degrees
1.  **MEng (Master of Engineering):** Professional, project-based. Good for jobs.
2.  **MSc (Master of Science):** Thesis-based. Good for PhD/Research. Harder to get into.

---

## 💰 The Reality Check
*   **Tuition:** ₹20 Lakh - ₹40 Lakh (Total). Cheaper than US.
*   **Housing Crisis:** Rent in Toronto/Vancouver is insanely high ($1500+ for a room).
*   **Part-time Jobs:** You can work 20 hours/week (often Starbucks/Uber) to pay living expenses.

---

## 🎓 Top Universities (The Ivy League of Canada)
1.  **University of Toronto (UofT):** World-class AI research (Hinton taught here).
2.  **UBC (Vancouver):** Beautiful campus, strong tech ties.
3.  **Waterloo:** The "MIT of Canada". Insane co-op placements.
4.  **McGill:** The Harvard of Canada.

---

## ⚠️ Warning
*   **Cold:** It gets to -30°C. Do not underestimate the winter depression.
*   **Job Market:** The tech market is 10x smaller than the US. Many people end up working for US companies remotely.
