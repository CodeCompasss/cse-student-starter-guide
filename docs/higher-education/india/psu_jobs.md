# 🏭 PSU Jobs through GATE
### "The Government Job Stability"

Not everyone wants to code for a startup. If you want **job security, perks, and respect**, PSUs (Public Sector Undertakings) are the goal.

---

## 🏢 Top PSUs Recruiting CSE
1.  **ONGC (Oil & Natural Gas):** High salary.
2.  **IOCL (Indian Oil Corp):** Massive perks.
3.  **POSOCO (Power System Operation):** Grid management.
4.  **DRDO / ISRO:** Research organizations (Scientist/Engineer roles).

---

## 🎯 The Strategy
*   **Rank:** You need a **Top 100 Rank** in GATE. Competition is brutal.
*   **Interview:** GATE score gets you the interview. The interview tests your core knowledge + personality.

---

## 🧪 Direct PhD (The Researcher Route)
Instead of M.Tech, you can join a **Direct PhD** after B.Tech.
*   **Eligibility:** High GATE score.
*   **Where:** Top IITs and IISc Bangalore.
*   **Stipend:** Higher than M.Tech (approx ₹31,000/month).
*   **Career:** Professor or R&D Scientist (Google Research, Microsoft Research).
*   **Warning:** It is a 5-6 year commitment. Don't do it just for the title.
