# 👔 MBA after Engineering
### "Moving from Code to Management"

Many engineers realize they don't love coding, but they love **Product, Strategy, and Money**. An MBA is the pivot point.

---

## 🚪 The Entrance Exams
1.  **CAT (Common Admission Test):** The king of exams. 99%ile is mandatory for GEM (General Engineer Male) candidates.
2.  **XAT (XLRI):** Slightly tougher than CAT, but gets you into XLRI.
3.  **GMAT:** For ISB (Indian School of Business) and foreign MBAs.

---

## 🏫 Top B-Schools for Engineers
1.  **IIM Ahmedabad/Bangalore/Calcutta (ABC):** The Holy Trinity.
2.  **ISB Hyderabad:**
    *   **Requirement:** Needs 2+ years of work experience.
    *   **Focus:** Very Tech/Product management focused.
3.  **FMS Delhi:** Extremely cheap fees (ROI is insane).

---

## 💼 Tech MBA vs General MBA
*   **Tech MBA:** A new trend. Focuses on managing software teams.
*   **Product Management:** The most common role for Engineers after MBA. You become the "CEO of the Product".

---

## ⚖️ The "Work Ex" Debate
*   **Fresher MBA:** Good if you are 100% sure you hate coding.
*   **MBA with Work Ex (2-3 years):** **Highly Recommended.**
    *   You understand how corporate works.
    *   You get better placements.
    *   ISB only takes people with experience.
