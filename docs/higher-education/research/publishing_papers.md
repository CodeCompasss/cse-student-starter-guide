# 📄 How to Publish Research Papers
### "The Currency of Academia"

If you want a Masters/PhD admit, a **Research Paper** is the strongest weapon you can have. It proves you can think, not just code.

---

## 🚫 The Scam Warning (Read First!)
Many "International Journals" are scams. They ask you to pay $50 and publish your paper in 2 days.
*   **Examples:** IJCRT, JETIR (Avoid these!).
*   **Why avoid?** Professors know these are fake. It will hurt your application more than help.

**Target:** **IEEE** or **ACM** Conferences / Journals. Or **Springer** / **Elsevier**.
*   **Rule:** If they ask you for money to *review* the paper, it's likely a scam.

---

## 🔬 How to Write a Paper (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: The Idea (Novelty)
You don't need to invent a new AI. You just need a small improvement.
*   *Bad:* "Face Recognition System". (Done 1 million times).
*   *Good:* "Face Recognition System for Low-Light Villages in India using Thermal Cameras". (Specific context).

### Step 2: The Mentor
You cannot do this alone.
*   Find a professor in your college who has a Google Scholar profile.
*   Ask to work under them.

### Step 3: The Structure (IMRAD)
1.  **Introduction:** What is the problem?
2.  **Methodology:** What is your algorithm/architecture? (Draw diagrams!).
3.  **Results:** Graphs proving your method is 2% better than existing ones.
4.  **Discussion:** Why did it work?

### Step 4: Submission
*   Find a conference (e.g., "IEEE Region 10 Conference").
*   Submit PDF.
*   Wait 2-3 months for "Peer Review".
