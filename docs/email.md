# 📧 Smart Email Setup (3 Email System)

The idea is simple:

> Don’t mix everything into one inbox. Separate based on importance + spam risk + career use.

---

## 🔐 1. PERSONAL EMAIL (Most Important)

This is your **safest and most protected email**.

### Use it for:

* Bank accounts 🏦
* Government services
* Aadhaar / passport / ID systems
* Insurance
* Tax / legal documents
* Password recovery for other emails

### Rules:

* Do NOT sign up randomly on websites
* Keep it private (only trusted usage)
* Never use it for spam, apps, or coding platforms

### Security setup (VERY IMPORTANT):

* Enable **2-Factor Authentication (2FA)** (Google Authenticator / Auth App preferred)
* Use a **strong unique password**
* Add **recovery email + recovery phone**
* Turn on **login alerts**
* Save backup codes offline (not in email itself)

👉 This is your “digital identity email”

---

## 📩 2. GENERAL EMAIL (Everyday Use)

This is your **normal internet email**.

### Use it for:

* Social media (Instagram, Discord, etc.)
* Shopping websites
* Apps and subscriptions
* YouTube, newsletters
* Random signups
* Personal communication

### Rules:

* Expect spam here (it’s normal)
* You can create filters/folders to manage noise
* Don’t connect this to government or banking stuff

### Security:

* 2FA recommended (but less strict than personal email)
* Good password still important
* Can be used on multiple devices

👉 Think of this as your “internet identity email”

---

## 💼 3. PLACEMENT / CAREER EMAIL

This is your **professional + career-focused email**

### Use it for:

* Job applications
* Internships
* LinkedIn + GitHub
* Hackathons
* Resume contact
* Company communication
* Referral requests

### Rules:

* Keep it clean (no random spam signups)
* Professional format like:

  * [firstname.lastname@gmail.com](mailto:firstname.lastname@gmail.com)
  * [firstname.dev@gmail.com](mailto:firstname.dev@gmail.com)
* No funny usernames

### Security:

* Strong password
* 2FA enabled
* Regularly check inbox (very important during placement season)

👉 This is your “career identity email”
